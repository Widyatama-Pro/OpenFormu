# OpenFormu Anti-Slop Design Direction (`[data-ui-mode="anti-slop"]`)

## 1. Scope and Authority

This document is the authoritative design direction for OpenFormu's third UI mode, **Anti-Slop** (`[data-ui-mode="anti-slop"]`), governed by `.agents/skills/antislop` and its companion skills (`antislop-ui`, `antislop-copywriting`, `antislop-human`, `antislop-layoutmobile`, `antislop-code`).

- **Relationship to `docs/DESIGN.md`:** `docs/DESIGN.md` is a Linear marketing analysis kept in the repository as an external reference only. Per `antislop` rule `R-30`, OpenFormu must not clone Linear, Vercel, Stripe, or Notion.
- **Mode Isolation and Persistence (`Biasa` and `Enhanced` untouched):** Every visual, typographic, elevation, motion, and contrast override derived from this direction is scoped strictly to `[data-ui-mode="anti-slop"]`. Canonical `UiMode = "biasa" | "enhanced" | "anti-slop"` (default `"enhanced"`) persists via `localStorage` key `openformu-ui-mode` (with legacy migration from `openformul-ui-mode`, `openformu-theme-enhancer`, and `openformul-theme-enhancer`), `UserPreferences.uiMode` in IndexedDB (`openformul-db`), and pre-paint hydration in `index.html` (`data-ui-mode` plus compatibility mirror `data-ui-enhancer`), keeping `Biasa` (`[data-ui-mode="biasa"]`) and `Enhanced` (`[data-ui-mode="enhanced"]`) visually and functionally intact without regression.

---

## 2. Design Read and Three Dials

> **Design Read:** Reading this as: Precision agritech feed-formulation workbench for livestock farmers and nutritionists, in a tactile field-ledger and laboratory-instrument style, dial `ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1`.

| Dial       | Value          | Operational Meaning in OpenFormu `Anti-Slop`                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **ENERGY** | `2` (Balanced) | Confident typographic authority on cost per kg, inclusion percentages, and LP feasibility status without agency theatrics or neon glows.                                         |
| **RHYTHM** | `2` (Balanced) | Structured tabular field-ledger grids punctuated by deliberate zone breaks (navigation rail, active target summary, ration matrix, and solver results).                          |
| **MOTION** | `1` (Calm)     | Crisp hover, active, and focus states plus discrete UI feedback only. Zero endless shimmer sweeps, zero infinite spotlight pulses, and full `prefers-reduced-motion` compliance. |

---

## 3. Five Liveliness Levers Applied to OpenFormu

1. **One focal point per screen:** Each route leads with the primary decision the user makes there (Formulation: solver feasibility and cost per kg beside the ration ledger; Ingredient DB: searchable 8-class nutrient matrix; Livestock Profiles: requirement standard table; Saved Recipes: ration ledger and PDF export; Advanced Modules: specialized ration tools; Settings: active display and engine configuration).
2. **Hierarchical contrast:** Tabular numbers (`tabular-nums`), clear typographic weight jumps on KPI figures, `1px` architectural hairline borders calibrated to `>= 3.0:1`, and 0-failure WCAG AA text contrast across all 5 palettes (`original-default`, `template-01`, `template-03`, `template-04`, `template-05`) in Light, Dark, and Dark AMOLED.
3. **Whitespace as structure:** Generous breathing room separates functional zones on desktop (`16px-20px` panel rhythm), tightening proportionally (`8px-12px` gutters) on mobile so data tables and numeric cells remain readable without horizontal overflow.
4. **One deliberate accent:** `--color-primary` is reserved for primary actions, active navigation indicators, `OpenFormu` wordmark emphasis, and dialog focus highlights (`PrecisionEngineDialog`), while `--focus-ring` (mapped in `src/theme/anti-slop.css` to calibrated `--color-accent` or high-contrast focus hues per palette) marks keyboard `:focus-visible` outlines rather than decorating resting cards and badges. (`[data-ui-mode="anti-slop"]` uses light/dark canvas surfaces rather than `--shell-chrome-*` dark chrome.)
5. **Identity motif ("Field Ledger & Instrument Grid"):** Crisp `1px` structural rules, aligned `tabular-nums` columns, the split `OpenFormu` wordmark, domain-mapped International Feed Number (`IFN`) `FeedClassIcon` markers, and a disciplined radius hierarchy.

## 5. Quality Locks, Radius Scale, and Copywriting Rules

### 5.1 Radius and Spacing Scale (`R-11`)

- **Inputs, table cells, badges:** `6px` (`rounded-md`) to anchor tabular data cleanly.
- **Buttons, navigation rows, tab segments:** `8px` (`rounded-lg`) for interactive touch targets (`>= 44x44px` on coarse/mobile viewports).
- **Cards, panels, drawers:** `12px` (`rounded-xl`) to frame distinct ledger zones without pill distortion.
- **Modal dialogs:** `12px` (`rounded-xl` on desktop / `rounded-t-xl` on mobile sheets) in `Anti-Slop`, contrasted with `16px` (`rounded-2xl` / `rounded-t-2xl`) in `Biasa` and `Enhanced` (`SheetDialog.tsx:137-147`), for clear ledger dialog framing.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Reserved strictly for semantic `Switch` tracks and thumbs (`Switch.tsx:46,56`, `ThemeSwitcherMenu.tsx:599,609`), small palette preview swatch dots and position indicator bars (`ThemeSwitcherMenu.tsx:367,435`), the `8px` solving indicator dot (`FormulationWorkbench.tsx:313`), thin `6px` inline bar tracks (`FormulationCharts.tsx:259-275`), composition breakdown bar and segment endcaps (`BatchMixerCalculator.tsx:146,166`), proximity meter track/fill/thumb (`NutrientTargetsPanel.tsx:851,857,865`), progress track/range/fill/thumb and node circles (`NeumorphicProgressTrack.tsx:54,60,70,82,96`), and circular selection dots inside dropdown option rows (`ThemedSelect.tsx:464`), never for cards, buttons, inputs, or status badges.

### 5.2 Copywriting and Content Locks (`R-02`, `R-15`, `R-16`, `R-17`, `R-18`, `R-36`, `R-38`)

- **Zero em dashes (`U+2014`):** Replaced with commas, colons, parentheses, or hyphens (`-` for empty numeric cells) in all `Anti-Slop` UI copy, tooltips, aria labels, and feed data copy.
- **Zero AI buzzwords or fake claims:** No marketing filler, invented stats, or fictional testimonials; all nutrient standards cite real SNI (22), Kearl 1982 (82), NRC 1988 (24), or Hartadi 1980 (304) references.
- **Action-specific CTAs and honest states (`R-27`):** Buttons state the concrete action (for example, opening shadow prices, saving a ration, resetting filters, adding a feed, or exporting a PDF), and data views render explicit Empty, Loading, and Error states with next actions.

### 5.3 Quality Locks and Craftsmanship Standard (`R-05`, `R-20`, `R-21`, `R-29`, `R-30`, `R-31`, `C-1..C-5`)

- **`R-05` (Layout & Page Structure):** Content-driven `RHYTHM 2` composition across all 6 routes (navigation rail/bottom bar, formulation ledger, target inspector, result summary, library tables, and settings), avoiding Hero+3-cards, bento mosaics, fake terminals, or 4-column marketing footers.
- **`R-20` & `R-30` (Visual Identity & Anti-Cloning):** Preserves OpenFormu's tactile `"Field Ledger & Instrument Grid"` identity even if the wordmark changes, and does not mimic `docs/DESIGN.md` (external Linear reference) or Vercel/Stripe/Notion.
- **`R-21` (Dark Mode):** Working Light, Dark, System, and Dark AMOLED Pure Black toggles verified across all 5 palettes in `Biasa`, `Enhanced`, and `Anti-Slop` (`index.html`, `use-theme.ts`, `android-theme-bridge.ts`).
- **`R-29` (Color Palette):** Each of the 5 curated palettes binds 2 to 3 core/semantic colors plus 1 accent and calibrated neutrals in `src/theme/anti-slop.css`.
- **`R-31` (Written Keystone Reasons):** One-line justifications are documented across color (`§3.4`, `R-01`), layout (`§3.1`, `R-05`, `R-14`), typography (`R-06`), spacing (`§3.3`, `§5.1`), cards (`R-12`, `R-14`), and icons/illustrations (`R-04`, `R-22`).
- **Craftsmanship Criteria (`C-1..C-5`):** Intentionality (`C-1`: zero default-only choices), Functional Completeness (`C-2`: zero dead controls; upcoming advanced items passively labeled `Segera Hadir` / `Coming Soon`), Content-Driven Composition (`C-3`), Resilience (`C-4`: Empty/Loading/Error states, 3 modes x Light/Dark/AMOLED, `320px-1440px` reflow, keyboard-only operation), and Evidence Over Claims (`C-5`).
